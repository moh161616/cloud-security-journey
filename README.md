# Cloud Security Journey

### From Exploration to Specialization.

Every serious journey begins with a decision.

For a long time, I explored different areas of cybersecurity, working through CTF challenges, experimenting with tools, and investigating vulnerabilities across different environments. I learned a great deal along the way, and I could solve challenges and understand the techniques behind them.

But there was one thing missing: **depth.**

I was exploring too many directions without committing to one. I could understand individual concepts, but building deep, interconnected expertise requires something more than exploration. It requires focus, structure, and consistency.

That realization led me to Cloud Computing.

This is no longer an attempt to explore another field. It is a deliberate commitment to building strong foundations in cloud engineering, understanding how cloud infrastructure works, mastering its architecture and services, and ultimately developing the expertise to secure complex cloud environments.

**I have chosen my direction, built my roadmap, and committed to following it.**

The journey is demanding, technically challenging, and intentionally structured. It goes beyond collecting certifications or completing tutorials. The objective is to develop practical engineering skills, understand systems at their core, and build the ability to design, operate, and secure cloud infrastructure.

### Why This Repository?

This repository documents that journey through a structured learning roadmap, technical notes, practical exercises, and carefully organized resources.

It is built not only as a personal knowledge base, but also as a resource for anyone serious about pursuing Cloud Engineering and eventually specializing in Cloud Security.

If you're considering this path, I encourage you to explore the repository. You may find something valuable here.

**A roadmap is only valuable when you follow it.**

I'm here to do exactly that.
